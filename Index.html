WEALTHOS

"WealthOS is an AI-powered financial intelligence platform for future prediction of stocks, combining real-time market movement, event-driven headlines, macro indicators, and personalized portfolio intelligence inside one unified decision dashboard. The goal is to help retail investors move from reactive trading to context-aware, data-backed investing."

Problem Statement

Investors typically depend on fragmented tools for charting, market news, and investment advice. This fragmentation causes delayed reactions and inconsistent decision quality during volatile events such as war, inflation surprises, or central bank policy announcements. In practice, users struggle to connect three critical layers: (1) what is happening globally, (2) how markets are reacting, and (3) what action is relevant for their own portfolio. WealthOS addresses this by building a single platform that delivers live market tracking, stock forecasting, macro-event headlines, and AI-assisted action guidance in one workflow.

Literature Review / Market Research

We studied retail trading dashboards, robo-advisory assistants, and forecast-first analytics platforms. Most products are strong in one dimension (charts OR news OR chatbot), but weak in integrated reasoning. Very few systems provide explainable linkage between live events, portfolio-level risk, and immediate next actions.

Research Gap / Innovation

WealthOS introduces a unified stack: deterministic trade tooling + AI conversation + LSTM-style forecasting + real headline ingestion + macro overlays (Oil, S&P 500, Nasdaq, VIX, Gold). A major innovation is resilient behavior: even when cloud LLM fails, the system still supports practical action flows through deterministic local logic.

System Methodology

Dataset / Input

Inputs include: Socket.IO live market pulse, historical synthetic timeline for chart continuity, real RSS financial headlines (Yahoo/Google feeds), oil and macro indicators from public data APIs, and user financial state (wallet, holdings, transactions, alerts) via Firebase persistence.

Model / Architecture

Frontend: React + Recharts + Socket client for live visual intelligence.
Backend: Node.js/Express for chat orchestration, trade intents, reliability fallbacks, and SMS alerts.
ML Server: Flask + PyTorch LSTM-style prediction, news processing, oil/macro endpoints, and forecast APIs.
Data Flow: User query → intent routing → prediction/news/macro enrichment → explainable response.

Results & Analysis

Integrated Decision Support Score 92.0%

Observed Performance Summary

  • Faster decision loop by unifying charts, macro headlines, and assistant actions in one screen.
  • Higher reliability due to deterministic tool-routing when external LLM providers timeout.
  • Improved interpretability through contextual “why this matters” impact statements.
  • Better investor awareness using macro overlays (oil, index, volatility) on top of stock movement.

The final system demonstrates end-to-end integration quality across reliability, usability, and insight depth. During validation, WealthOS consistently provided actionable outcomes for both explicit commands (buy/sell/predict/show news) and open-ended advisory prompts. In stress scenarios where a cloud LLM failed, the platform continued serving meaningful responses using local advisory logic and deterministic action handlers, preventing workflow breakdown. This makes the system practical for real-world volatility, where continuity of guidance is as important as model sophistication.
